# Number Battle Royale

> A pure vanilla JavaScript & SVG minimalist physics game. Break the exponentially growing wall before the 20-second timer runs out.

## 🎮 Core Gameplay
You are a pure white ball trapped in a 100x100 arena. Every level has a "Wall" with an absurd amount of HP. 
- **The Rule**: Bounce off the walls to deal damage. 
- **The Timer**: You have exactly **20 seconds** per level. 
- **The Goal**: Reduce the wall's HP to zero before time runs out. If you fail, you die.
- **Progression**: Clear the level, reset the timer, and face an even larger number. How deep can you go?

## 📈 Extreme Big Numbers
From `1` to `1e(1e100)`, the wall's HP scales to astronomical levels. 
This game features a **custom scientific notation and exponent-tower engine** built entirely from scratch. No native `BigInt`, no `Infinity` crashes—just pure mathematical destruction.

## 🔮 The Arsenal
Every run, you can choose from a variety of unique arithmetic-based balls. 
*(We are constantly adding new mechanics!)*

- **The Basics**: Steady damage and simple bounces.
- **The Multipliers**: Damage that grows exponentially with every single wall bounce.
- **The Shredders**: Balls that manipulate the wall's HP directly (e.g., halving or square-rooting the current health).
- **The Tragic Ones**: Balls that sacrifice everything... including their own movement speed.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Logic & Physics**: Pure Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Rendering**: SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) for pixel-perfect, high-performance visuals.
- **Styling**: Pure CSS. No frameworks, no dependencies.
